# cost-analysis
Interactive cost analysis tool for Priority Healthcare client meetings.

**It's one file: `index.html`.** Fonts, logo and charts are all built in, so it works offline. You don't need internet, an install or a login.

## Get your offline copy
1. Download `index.html` from this repo (or click **Download a blank copy** at the bottom of the tool).
2. Save it anywhere, like your desktop or a shared drive. Double-click it to open it in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.

## What's inside
- **Temporary staffing**: current agency rates vs Priority by role. The table has an "Other charges" row for travel, mileage, admin and minimum-shift fees. There's a 12-month, 3-year and 5-year cost chart, and a 5-year outlook with an adjustable rate-hold term. The 1:1 section compares your own PSW/HCA (straight time or overtime) with Priority, including statutory burden and optional benefits.
- **Temp-to-perm**: trial on Priority's rate, then a conversion fee (% of salary or flat, optionally scaling to $0 with hours worked), then your own payroll. It compares that path with staying on the current agency and with staying on Priority temp. It shows the month it becomes cheaper than agency and the fee payback month.
- **Province switch** (Ontario / Alberta) changes the statutory burden and role names.

## Saving work
- Edits autosave in your browser.
- **Save scenario** downloads a copy of the tool with every figure filled in, e.g. `Priority Cost Analysis - Maple Grove LTC - 2026-11-12.html`. Open it later, or send it to the client, and it reopens exactly as you left it.
- **Save as PDF** prints the current tab.
